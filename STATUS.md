- Drought availability fix shipped on local `dev` and `origin/dev` by merging `feat/risk-drought-availability` (base `189e393`), 2026-10-11. Not deployed. Drought archive calls send `start_date` and `end_date` for 90 inclusive dates in `Asia/Karachi`. The end date is the earlier of the Asia/Karachi day and the UTC day; Open-Meteo documents Best Match as available "to present" and does not define present as the Karachi date. A date-range HTTP 400 that names a real earlier latest day gets one recovery request; other 400s are not recovered, and a failed recovery leaves both rainfall totals null. The learned end date lasts for the existing 3-hour weather cache. A window is stored only when each requested date in it appears once with a numeric value, so a gap in older days can drop the 90-day total and still keep a complete 30-day total. Genuine zero stays zero. `weather_unavailable` still means a critical hazard input is missing, including missing drought history. The reason prefix and Risk Map banner say the drought assessment is limited when forecast max temperature is present and a 30-day or 90-day total is missing; they say some required weather is unavailable when other metrics remain; they say static NDMA context only when no usable weather exists. The drought prompt forbids treating 3-day rain as a persistent deficit, calling an absolute total a deficit without a baseline, and calling soil moisture "critically low" without a threshold. Scoring bands, Gemini-then-Grok provider order, the 10-second Open-Meteo timeout, the retry count, the 3-hour weather cache, and the 15-minute response cache are unchanged.
